Strength Log 

    

      
    
        Submit banks the session on this device and moves to the next day. It counts
        toward the volume bands and becomes the "last week" reference for next time - but
        the strength index, per-lift trends, bridge, stalls and adherence come from
        analyze.py, so those only move once you Export the rows into
        log.csv and re-run it. Nothing here is written to
        log.csv directly. Loads come from the double-progression rule in
        config.yaml, never from anywhere else. Weight only - reps are always
        the target shown, RIR always blank, so a deload can't trigger from what's logged
        here.
